# Glossary

## Claim

A proposition extracted from source evidence that the system can accept, reject, leave unresolved or find out of scope.

## Evidence

The exact source text and source metadata that support a claim.

## Methodology

A replaceable approach used to perform a pipeline task, such as claim extraction or entity resolution.

## Ontology

The concepts and relationships used to represent accepted knowledge, including their recorded history.

## Plan

The configured sequence of pipeline work that carries data from evidence to an observable result.

## Resolver

A methodology that determines whether a mention refers to an existing entity, a new provisional entity or no known entity.

## Source document

One GOV.UK content item and its retained source representation.
